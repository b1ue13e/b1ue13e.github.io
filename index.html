What connects
my work

My research sits between statistical learning and operations research. I'm interested in the decisions around a model as much as the predictions it produces: what to learn, when to reason, and which feedback to trust.

A

Decision learning & optimization

Sequential feedback, preference learning, strategic decision support, and learning-guided search for constrained problems.

B

Reasoning & agents

The value of additional computation, reliable feedback, and the design of agents that improve through experimentation.

C

Reliable machine learning

Representation dynamics, imperfect structural priors, and robust learning in changing temporal environments.

04 /

Selected research

A selection of ongoing and recent projects. Expand a project to explore the question behind it.

01

The value of reasoning

Allocating inference-time computation according to its decision value.

Reasoning
+

When does another reasoning step help enough to justify its cost? I study the distinction between uncertainty and the value of additional computation, with attention to stopping and allocation decisions.

02

Learning from unreliable feedback

Understanding how semantic feedback changes a learning or reasoning trajectory.

Reasoning
+

Feedback can guide an agent while also steering it away from its original objective. My current projects examine sequential semantic feedback, feedback poisoning, and optimization from textual advice.

03

Learning-guided optimization

Neural search and automated experimentation for mixed-integer optimization.

Decisions
+

I explore how learned guidance can improve search in constrained optimization, including neural large neighborhood search for sequential MINLP and value-guided research search for MIP.

04

Preference learning & decision support

Interactive advice, AHP consistency, and sequential multi-criteria sorting.

Decisions
+

How should a decision-support method respond when judgments evolve or conflict? My work on AHP and FlowSort studies the relationship between preference information, consistency, and useful decisions.

05

Strategic multi-criteria decisions

Bilevel decision support when participants respond strategically.

Decisions
+

I study how an institution can allocate limited support when participants have private information and incentives to shape their reports. The emphasis is on the interaction between preferences, incentives, and actual decisions.

06

Structural priors under uncertainty

Reliable temporal learning when assumed structure is incomplete or wrong.

Reliable ML
+

Domain knowledge can guide a model, but its assumptions can also be misspecified. I investigate prior reliability, recovery after structural shifts, and the distinction between sensor changes and genuine operating changes.

07

Representation & generalization

Spectral geometry, representation dynamics, and late generalization.

Reliable ML
+

My work on grokking and spectral diagnostics examines how learned representations change during training, and how those changes relate to generalization and reliability.
